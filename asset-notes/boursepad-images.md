# BoursePad portfolio images

These are AI-generated presentation images based on BoursePad's official store
screenshots, not unmodified app captures. They replace the previous speculative
mockups in the hero, featured project, and résumé experience gallery.

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen` tool, reference-guided generation, one
request per image. The [exact prompt set](boursepad-image-prompts.json) is saved
alongside this file. Each final image is 941 × 1672 pixels, approximately 9:16.

## Final assets

| Asset in `assets/images/` | Official reference | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [boursepad-discovery-store-v2.png](../assets/images/boursepad-discovery-store-v2.png) | App Store, screenshot 1 | Scholarship discovery and home navigation |
| [boursepad-scholarship-store-v2.png](../assets/images/boursepad-scholarship-store-v2.png) | Google Play, screenshot 6 | External scholarship detail |
| [boursepad-verification-store-v2.png](../assets/images/boursepad-verification-store-v2.png) | Google Play, screenshot 8 | Document verification guidance |

The images preserve the visible product structure and colors, while removing
the store-poster surroundings and phone hardware because the portfolio supplies
its own phone frames. Personal greetings and identity-document numbers are
omitted. The scholarship names, dates, amounts, and status in the reference are
historical store examples, not claims that a scholarship is currently available.
